# Thumbnail Prompt Template

## Master Formula

```
[SUBJECT] in [LOCATION], captured in [CAMERA STYLE], mood of [EMOTION],
with [DETAILS], [CULTURE ENERGY], [ATMOSPHERE], cinematic realism,
high CTR YouTube thumbnail, sharp focus, premium composition,
emotional realism, designed to stop scrolling, mobile optimized --ar 16:9
```

---

## Field Reference

### SUBJECT
Who is in the frame (or implied to be).
- young Black creative producer
- stylish Black artist
- group of [ethnicity] [adjective] friends
- [role] in [state/action]
- POV (no visible subject)

### LOCATION
Where the scene takes place.
- private Harlem loft
- Buffalo Route 33 at night
- museum hallway after hours
- luxury townhouse kitchen
- underground parking garage
- backseat driving through downtown

### CAMERA STYLE
What camera created this image.
- disposable flash camera
- 35mm film grain
- VHS camcorder
- old iPhone footage
- CCTV security camera
- Arri Alexa cinematic
- paparazzi zoom lens
- magazine editorial flash

### EMOTION / MOOD
The emotional register of the image.
- mysterious and culturally elite
- dangerous calm
- jazz psychedelic elegance
- private culture and rare access
- nostalgic and emotionally rich
- weird but tasteful

### DETAILS
Specific visual elements that make the scene real.
- dim gold lighting, champagne glasses, jazz energy
- rain on windshield, city lights glowing
- one spotlight, red velvet room
- expensive watches visible, warm shadows

### CULTURE ENERGY
Brand-specific culture tag.
- Black culture energy (NightWire)
- [producer-specific culture tag]

### ATMOSPHERE
Genre/vibe descriptor.
- jazz psychedelic trap atmosphere (NightWire)
- [producer-specific atmosphere]

---

## Ending Tags (always include)
```
cinematic realism, high CTR YouTube thumbnail, sharp focus, premium composition,
emotional realism, designed to stop scrolling, mobile optimized --ar 16:9
```
