# Thumbnail Winning Formulas

## The 7-Element Thumbnail

Every high-CTR thumbnail needs:

1. **Human energy** — a person, or clear human presence implied
2. **Story hint** — viewer wants to know what happens next
3. **Wealth/culture clue** — context that communicates quality
4. **Strong lighting** — one clear light source or dramatic gradient
5. **Emotion or curiosity** — a face, a gesture, or a situation that creates feeling
6. **Mobile readability** — readable at 1-inch thumbnail size
7. **Cinematic realism** — not obviously AI, not obviously stock photo

## Style Bucket Formulas

### MindLoft Sessions
- **Setting:** Private Harlem loft or hidden room
- **Lighting:** Warm gold, dim, intimate
- **Camera:** Disposable flash (authentic) or editorial flash (elevated)
- **People:** Group or single, stylish Black creatives
- **Hook:** What are they doing in that room? Why is it private?
- **Formula:** `[private moment] + [expensive detail] + [flash authenticity]`

### Buffalo Noir
- **Setting:** Buffalo streets, Route 33, night
- **Lighting:** City lights, cold blues, wet reflections
- **Camera:** VHS or old iPhone
- **People:** Lone figure or driver POV
- **Hook:** Something about this city feels dangerous and beautiful
- **Formula:** `[real city] + [night movement] + [cold grain]`

### Jazz Smoke
- **Setting:** Jazz club, bar, velvet room
- **Lighting:** One spotlight, amber, intimate
- **Camera:** 35mm film grain
- **People:** Solo subject, emotional state
- **Hook:** This person carries something heavy and beautiful
- **Formula:** `[closed space] + [lone subject] + [warm grain] + [smoke/shadow]`

### Harlem Private Society
- **Setting:** Brownstones, lofts, private parties
- **Lighting:** Warm flash, ambient
- **Camera:** Editorial flash, disposable
- **People:** Group, candid moments
- **Hook:** This is a world that doesn't post about itself
- **Formula:** `[cultural gathering] + [authentic candid] + [warmth]`

### Museum Nights
- **Setting:** Galleries, hallways, cultural spaces after hours
- **Lighting:** Security lights, spotlit art
- **Camera:** CCTV or editorial
- **People:** Black creative in cultural space
- **Hook:** You're not supposed to be here
- **Formula:** `[rare access] + [cultural space] + [after hours energy]`

### Street Prestige
- **Setting:** Parking garages, corners, streets
- **Lighting:** Neon, surveillance, fluorescent
- **Camera:** CCTV or paparazzi zoom
- **People:** Arriving, departing, in motion
- **Hook:** Something powerful just happened here
- **Formula:** `[street setting] + [power signal] + [grainy authenticity]`

## What Makes Thumbnails Fail

- No human energy (too abstract)
- Too dark to see at thumbnail size
- Too much text covering the image
- Generic luxury (sports cars, stacks of cash)
- AI faces that look fake
- No emotional hook
- Cluttered composition
- Color palette with no contrast

## The Mobile Test
Always preview at 1.5 inches wide. If the emotion doesn't read at that size: reject it.
