## CTR-Boosting Title Elements

**Include:**
- [FREE] or "Free Beat" or "Free Download"
- Current year (e.g. 2026)
- Specific emotion words (velvet, danger, private, hidden)
- Location specificity (Harlem, Buffalo, Miami)
- Artist name comparisons (tasteful)

**Avoid:**
- All caps screaming titles
- Generic phrases ("fire beat", "hard beat")
- Too many emojis
- Clickbait without visual payoff

## Title → CTR Score Logic

Scoring heuristics used by the system (0–100):
- Contains [FREE]: +15
- "type beat" present: +10
- Specific year: +8
- Emotional adjective: +12
- Location specific: +10
- 50–80 characters: +10
- Proper capitalization: +5
- Curiosity gap: +15
- Forbidden words (generic, corny): -20
