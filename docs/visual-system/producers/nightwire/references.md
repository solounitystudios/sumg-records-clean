## What Goes In Each Folder

### loft/
Hidden rooms, Harlem lofts, private lounges, luxury decay interiors.
Dark velvet, warm lamps, bookshelves, art on walls, dim parties.
Think: someone's private world that most people will never enter.

### buffalo/
Route 33, snow streets, wet pavement, gas stations, Buffalo skyline.
Old city lights, cold clear nights, steam from grates.
Think: real city, not glamorized — but still prestige.

### luxury/
Townhouse kitchens, velvet rooms, marble interiors, upscale villas.
Champagne glasses, expensive tasteful objects, candlelight.
Think: wealth you could miss if you weren't looking carefully.

### culture/
Museums, galleries, jazz halls, Black art spaces, cultural events.
Statues, paintings on walls, people in reverent spaces.
Think: cultural ownership, not just visiting.

### neon/
Purple/blue/crimson lighting, reflections on wet surfaces, city blur.
Windows, headlights, signs — light as texture and mood.
Think: color as emotion, not just decoration.

### vhs/
Old phone photos, 1990s camcorder grain, disposable camera flash.
Warm shifts, saturation artifacts, imperfect framing.
Think: the camera couldn't quite capture the moment, but tried.

### museum/
After-hours gallery moments, statues in low light, quiet hallways.
Security lights, marble floors, being alone in a massive space.
Think: access you're not supposed to have.

### party/
Black stylish friends, cocktails, games, candid laughter, chaotic elegance.
Donut moments, gin and tonics, beer pong in mansions.
Think: joy as its own form of cultural prestige.

### street-prestige/
Cars, garages, roads, night movement, city realism.
Surveillance cameras, headlights, motion blur.
Think: the street isn't separate from the life — it's part of it.

### creative-power/
Producer on set, camera flashes, studio sessions, editorial authority.
Behind-the-scenes creative leadership, visual excellence.
Think: this person builds worlds.

---

## Reference Image Rules

### Use:
- Midjourney winners that match NightWire DNA
- YouTube producer thumbnail winners with high CTR
- Personal photos that capture the right energy
- Textures (velvet, marble, concrete, film grain)
- Street photography from Harlem, Buffalo, Miami
- Fashion/editorial references that match the palette
- Film stills from movies with the right visual energy

### Never use:
- Random low-quality images
- Off-brand anime or cartoon styles
- Generic trap backgrounds
- Low-taste luxury photos (chains on white backgrounds)
- Anything that breaks NightWire identity
- Stock photos that feel commercial and fake

## Visual Reference Films / Photography

These have the right energy even if content differs:

**Film:**
- *Moonlight* (Barry Jenkins) — intimacy, light, Black masculinity
- *Waves* (Trey Edward Shults) — neon, emotion, Florida youth
- *If Beale Street Could Talk* (Barry Jenkins) — Harlem warm grain
- *The Photograph* (Stella Meghie) — Black creative world
- *Malcolm & Marie* (Sam Levinson) — black and white cinematic intimacy

**Photography:**
- Gordon Parks (Harlem street, cultural prestige)
- Jamel Shabazz (authentic Black street culture)
- Renell Medrano (fashion-adjacent cultural intimacy)
- Tyler Mitchell (joyful Black youth in luxury settings)

**Music Videos:**
- Frank Ocean — intimate, strange, beautiful
- SZA — real natural Black female world
- Kendrick Lamar *HUMBLE.* — cinematic realism
- Jay-Z *Picasso Baby* — museum energy
