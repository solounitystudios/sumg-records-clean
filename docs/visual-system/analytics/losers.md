# Thumbnail Losers

## How to Define a Loser

CTR below 2% in first 24 hours, or below 2.5% after 7 days on a video with 1000+ impressions.

## Common Failure Patterns

Watch for these and eliminate from rotation:

- Abstract images with no human element → low CTR
- Dark thumbnails with no light source → unreadable at small size
- Generic studio laptop shots → blend into feed
- Text-heavy thumbnails → hard to read mobile
- Overly AI-looking faces → distrust signal
- Sports car backgrounds without people → fake luxury
