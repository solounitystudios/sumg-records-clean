# Thumbnail Winners

## How to Define a Winner

CTR above 6% in first 24 hours, or above 4% sustained after 7 days.

Track: style bucket, camera style, mood, title pairing.

## Patterns to Watch For

Once 10+ winners are logged, look for:
- Which style bucket wins most often?
- Which camera style drives clicks?
- Which title/thumbnail combinations compound?
- Do certain moods outperform others on certain days?
- Does beat genre affect thumbnail CTR?
